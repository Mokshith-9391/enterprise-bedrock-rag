# VPN Guide

Document owner: IT Infrastructure. Last reviewed: February 2026.

## When you need the VPN
Use the VPN whenever you access internal systems (HR portal, Expenses app, Git server, internal wikis) from outside an office network, including from home.

## Setting up the VPN on a company laptop
1. Open the Company Portal app and install "Secure Access Client".
2. Enter the server address vpn.example-corp.in.
3. Sign in with your company email and password.
4. Approve the multi-factor authentication (MFA) prompt in the Authenticator app.
5. The client shows "Connected" and a green shield icon.

## Personal devices
Personal devices cannot connect to the VPN. Use the browser-based access portal for email and chat only.

## Troubleshooting
- "Authentication failed": check your password has not expired, then retry the MFA prompt.
- Slow connection: switch to the nearest gateway (Mumbai or Hyderabad) in the client settings.
- Still not working: raise a ticket in the IT Service Desk under "Network > VPN". Priority P3 tickets are answered within 4 business hours.
