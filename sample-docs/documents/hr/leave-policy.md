# Leave Policy

Document owner: People Operations. Effective: 1 April 2026. Applies to all permanent employees in India.

## 1. Annual leave
- Every permanent employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Leave accrues at 2 days per completed month of service.
- Employees who join mid-year receive leave on a pro-rata basis from their joining month.

## 2. Carry forward and encashment
- Up to 10 unused annual leave days can be carried forward into the next calendar year.
- Days above 10 lapse on 31 December unless the employee's manager approved a written exception before 30 November.
- Carried-forward days can be encashed only at the time of resignation or retirement, at the basic salary rate.

## 3. Sick leave
- Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave cannot be carried forward.
- A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive working days.

## 4. Parental leave
- Birthing parents receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave, in line with the Maternity Benefit Act.
- Non-birthing parents receive 10 working days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 3 months of the birth or adoption.

## 5. How to apply
1. Apply in the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance for planned leave of 3 days or more.
2. Your reporting manager approves or rejects within 2 working days.
3. Unplanned sick leave must be recorded in the portal on the day you return to work.

## 6. Public holidays
The company observes 12 public holidays per year. The list is published in the HR portal each December.
