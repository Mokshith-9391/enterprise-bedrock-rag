# Work From Home Policy

Document owner: People Operations. Effective: 1 January 2026.

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed their 3-month probation may work from home (WFH) up to 3 days per week.
Roles that require on-site hardware access (network operations, facilities, lab engineering) are limited to 1 WFH day per week.

## Core hours
Employees working from home must be reachable on chat and phone between 10:00 and 16:00 IST.

## Equipment and allowance
- The company provides a laptop and headset. Monitors can be borrowed from the office for home use.
- Employees receive a monthly home-internet allowance of INR 1,500, paid with salary.

## Security requirements
- Company systems may only be accessed from home through the company VPN, as described in the IT VPN Guide.
- Working from public Wi-Fi (cafes, airports) is allowed only when connected to the VPN.
- Printing confidential documents at home is not permitted.

## Requesting WFH
Record planned WFH days in the HR portal by Friday for the following week. Managers may recall employees to the office with 24 hours' notice for business needs.
