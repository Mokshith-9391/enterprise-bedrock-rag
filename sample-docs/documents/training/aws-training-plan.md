# AWS Skills Training Plan 2026

Document owner: Learning and Development.

## Who should attend
All engineers joining cloud, DevOps or data teams complete this plan in their first 90 days.

## Learning path
1. Weeks 1-2: AWS Cloud Practitioner fundamentals (IAM, EC2, S3, VPC basics).
2. Weeks 3-6: Solutions Architect Associate preparation with hands-on labs.
3. Weeks 7-10: Specialisation track: DevOps (CodePipeline, Terraform, EKS) or Generative AI (Amazon Bedrock, Knowledge Bases, RAG design).
4. Weeks 11-12: Capstone project reviewed by a senior engineer.

## Certification support
- The company pays the exam fee for one associate-level AWS certification per year.
- A second attempt is funded if the first attempt scores within 50 points of the passing score.
- Engineers receive 2 paid study days before the exam.

## Lab accounts
Training lab accounts have a monthly budget of USD 50 per learner. Resources are deleted automatically every Sunday at 23:00 IST.
