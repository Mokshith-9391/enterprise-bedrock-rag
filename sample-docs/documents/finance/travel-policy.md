# Domestic Travel Policy

Document owner: Finance. Effective: 1 April 2026.

## Travel class
- Flights: economy class for all employees on domestic routes. Book at least 7 days in advance where possible.
- Trains: AC 2-tier for journeys up to 12 hours.

## Hotel limits per night (including taxes)
| City category | Cities | Limit per night |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Metro | Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata | INR 7,000 |
| Tier 2 | Pune, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Kochi, Chandigarh and similar | INR 5,000 |
| Other | All other locations | INR 3,500 |

Stays above the limit need prior written approval from a department head.

## Daily allowance (per diem)
- Metro cities: INR 2,000 per day for meals and local incidentals.
- All other cities: INR 1,500 per day.
- Per diem is not paid for days when meals are provided by a client or conference.

## Local transport
Taxis and ride-hailing apps are reimbursed at actual cost with a receipt. Airport transfers are always reimbursable.

## Approvals
All travel must be approved in the travel portal by the reporting manager before booking.
