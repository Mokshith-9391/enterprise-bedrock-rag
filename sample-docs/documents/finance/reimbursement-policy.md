# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Document owner: Finance. Effective: 1 April 2026.

## What can be claimed
Business travel, client meals, approved training fees, and work-related purchases under INR 10,000.
Purchases of INR 10,000 or more must go through Procurement, not reimbursement.

## Deadlines
- Submit claims within 30 days of the expense date. Claims older than 60 days are rejected.
- Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary run.

## Receipts
- An itemised receipt or GST invoice is required for every expense above INR 500.
- For expenses below INR 500 without a receipt, a self-declaration is accepted, up to INR 2,000 per month.

## Client meals
Client meals are reimbursed up to INR 2,500 per attendee. List every attendee and their company in the claim.

## How to submit
1. Open the Expenses app and choose "New claim".
2. Attach receipts as PDF or photo.
3. Select the cost centre and the approving manager.
4. Finance reviews within 5 working days.
